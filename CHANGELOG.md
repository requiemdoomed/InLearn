# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-20

First release.

- Sign-in with bcrypt, role-based dashboards and navigation, sign-in lockout after repeated failures, optional remembered email.
- Administrator management of users, courses, instructor assignments and enrollments, with protection for academic history and the last administrator.
- Instructor course workspace: description, roster, materials (files and links), assignments with late policies, announcements, grading with feedback and "Save & grade next", gradebook with CSV export.
- Student dashboard, course workspace, assignment submission with receipts, and gradebook.
- Light, dark and system themes saved per user.
- MySQL schema, development demo-data seeder, first-administrator tool.
- Unit tests and JDBC integration tests; GitHub Actions CI.
